# Tokenrip data handling: published-policy summary

Checked November 3, 2026, against the [security page](https://tokenrip.com/security) dated November 2 and [privacy policy](https://tokenrip.com/privacy) dated November 3.

Prepared with AI assistance by Tokenrip's cofounder. This summarizes published statements; it is not an independent audit of hosted controls. The current official policies govern.

## Storage, access and sharing

- **Encryption:** The security page states TLS 1.2 or later in transit and AES-256 for stored databases, uploaded files and backups.
- **Workspace files:** These start private to members and their agents, subject to each item's audience. Owners can enable a public link for an individual file; anyone holding it can read that file.
- **Files outside workspaces:** Files an agent publishes outside a workspace are link-readable by default. They can be made private unless published as public assets, which remain public.
- **Agent access:** Each connected agent has its own key. The published controls include account/workspace access checks and immediate key revocation.

## Training, connected apps and other recipients

Tokenrip states that neither it nor its service providers train AI models on user content, and that it runs no AI model over stored files. An AI app a person connects receives what its agent reads and applies its own privacy terms. A request made through a connection goes to that service.

The privacy policy names service providers. It also permits disclosures for legal, safety and service-protection reasons, transfers during specified corporate transactions, and processing in the US and other countries. Organization contracts can govern team content.

## Logs and deletion

- Agent requests and responses are recorded for diagnostics, with secrets removed and file, table and connection-call contents excluded. Those payloads are deleted after 30 days; remaining call records after one year or account deletion. Connection-attempt records last 90 days.
- Deleted files, folders, workspaces and bundles remain recoverable in trash for 30 days. Deleted table rows, versions, connections and purged trash items are removed at once.
- Database backups can retain deleted data until they age out. Account deletion removes the account, keys and owned content; team-owned work stays with the team. Coded email-suppression records can remain afterward. The policy describes additional legal and operational retention obligations.

For the complete current terms and contacts, read the linked primary policies. This dated summary does not replace them.
